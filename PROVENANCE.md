The maintainer has not accepted them, and the run does not prove the best possible packings.

`/data/adp/investigations/record_forge_2_packing_ramsey_2026-08-30_v1/packing/certificates/`

`/data/adp/scripts/codex_record_forge_2_packing_verify.py`
